# HR Attrition Dashboard

This repository contains an **HR Attrition Dashboard** built in **Power BI**. The dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of employee attrition, focusing on various factors such as demographics, turnover trends, and employee wellness. It is designed to help HR professionals understand and address key drivers of employee turnover.

## Dashboard Overview

The HR Attrition Dashboard is divided into four key sections:

### 1. Demographics
This dashboard focuses on the demographic aspects of employee attrition, including:

- **Female Employee Attrition**: Analysis of attrition trends among female employees.
- **Male Employee Attrition**: Analysis of attrition trends among male employees.
- **Total Attrition by Various Fields**: A comprehensive overview of attrition across multiple demographic parameters.
- **Sum of Attrition Count by Age**: Insights into how attrition varies across different age groups.
- **Sum of Attrition by Overtime**: Examines the impact of overtime on employee attrition.
- **Sum of Attrition Count by Distance from Home**: Analyzes attrition based on employees' proximity to their workplace.
- **Sum of Attrition by Marital Status**: Investigates the correlation between marital status and attrition.

### 2. Turnover Analysis 1
This section highlights turnover trends across job roles and departments:

- **Total Attrition by Job Role**: Identifies job roles with the highest attrition rates.
- **Total Attrition by Department**: Examines attrition trends within different departments.
- **Total Attrition by Business Level and Gender**: Analyzes attrition at different business levels, segmented by gender.
- **Total Attrition by Years in Control**: Shows how years of service impact attrition rates.

### 3. Turnover Analysis 2
This dashboard explores financial and performance-related factors of attrition:

- **Monthly Income and Attrition by Job Role**: Analyzes how monthly income influences attrition within various job roles.
- **Total Attrition by Overtime**: Examines the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.
- **Total Attrition by Job Level**: Insights into attrition trends by job level.
- **Total Attrition by Age and Gender**: A detailed view of age- and gender-specific attrition patterns.
- **Total Attrition by Performance Rating**: Highlights the impact of performance ratings on attrition.

### 4. Employee Wellness
This section evaluates attrition in relation to employee satisfaction and work-life balance:

- **Total Attrition by Environmental Satisfaction**: Investigates the role of workplace environment in employee retention.
- **Total Attrition by Job Improvement**: Examines how job improvements affect attrition.
- **Total Attrition by Performance Rating**: Insights into the link between performance ratings and attrition.
- **Relationship Satisfaction**: Analyzes attrition based on employee relationship satisfaction.
- **Job Satisfaction**: Highlights the impact of job satisfaction on attrition rates.
- **Total Attrition by Work-Life Balance**: Examines the balance between work and personal life in relation to attrition.

## Conclusion

The HR Attrition Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee attrition, helping organizations identify patterns and make informed decisions. By addressing factors such as demographics, turnover trends, and employee wellness, the dashboard serves as a valuable tool for improving retention strategies and fostering a healthier workplace environment.
